Privacy policy

This policy describes Daniel Odicho’s personal clawd Google integration and its authorized local tools.

Information accessed and purpose

Depending on the permissions granted and the task requested, the integration accesses Google account identity, email and attachments, calendar events, contacts, Drive files, and document and spreadsheet contents. It uses that information to carry out the account holder’s requested tasks.

Storage and service providers

Local tools store authorization credentials using their configured credential storage. Task outputs may be saved locally or in the destination the operator requests. When the operator uses an assistant or connected service, the information included in that workflow is also processed under that service’s own terms and privacy controls. This integration is not a public data-collection service.

Use and sharing

Google data is used for the requested workflows, not sold or used for advertising. Data is shared with a destination only as needed for an authorized task. The integration’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Control and retention

You can stop future access by removing clawd from Google Account connections. Revoking access does not automatically delete previously saved files or assistant conversations. Delete these through the applicable local tool or service. Contact the operator for questions about retained task artifacts or deletion.
