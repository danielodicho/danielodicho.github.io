clawd

clawd is Daniel Odicho’s personal integration for working with his Google accounts through authorized local command-line tools and assistant workflows. It is intended for the operator’s own accounts, not public sign-ups.

What it does

When authorized, these tools can help read and organize email, manage calendar events and contacts, and work with Drive files, Docs, and Sheets. Access is limited to the permissions granted by the account holder. Sending messages and changing account data follow the operator’s task instructions.

Account access

Google handles sign-in and consent. clawd does not ask for your Google password. Access can be revoked in your Google Account’s third-party connections settings.
